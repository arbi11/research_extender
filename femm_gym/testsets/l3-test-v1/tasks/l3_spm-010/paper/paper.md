# Design of a 18-slot, 16-pole surface permanent-magnet machine for a circulation pump

## Abstract

This paper describes a 18-slot, 16-pole surface permanent-magnet machine with a double-layer concentrated winding for a circulation pump. It gives the machine's dimensions, winding and materials, and two-dimensional finite-element results for the no-load back-EMF and the torque at the rated current.

## 1. Introduction

Fractional-slot concentrated windings give short end windings and low cogging torque, and they suit machines with many poles. Surface-mounted magnets keep the rotor simple. This paper records one such design in enough detail for its finite-element results to be reproduced.

## 2. Machine geometry

The stator has 18 parallel-sided teeth, each ending in a tooth tip whose underside is flat and at right angles to the tooth centre line; the slots between them have an arc-shaped bottom. The slot opening between adjacent tips is air, and each slot holds two coil sides side by side. The rotor carries 16 arc-shaped magnets bonded to the surface of a cylindrical rotor core, with no retaining sleeve and air between neighbouring magnets. Table 1 gives the main dimensions.

The slot bottom is an arc of radius 63.7 mm. The underside of each tooth tip lies 1.9 mm beyond the bore, measured along the tooth centre line. The air gap between the magnet surface and the stator bore is 0.65 mm. The rated speed is 3000 r/min.

## 3. Winding

The winding is a double-layer concentrated winding: each of the 18 teeth carries one coil, so every slot holds one coil side from each of its two teeth. The coils are connected into three phases by the star of slots, for the highest fundamental winding factor. All coils have the same number of turns, and the 6 coils of each phase form 2 identical parallel paths; Table 1 gives the turns in series in one path.

## 4. Materials

The stator core is stacked from M-22 14 mil laminations. The rotor core is laminated. It is mounted on a non-magnetic shaft. The magnets have a remanence of 1.31 T and a relative recoil permeability of 1.05. Each magnet is magnetized radially, alternately outward and inward.

## 5. Results

A two-dimensional magnetostatic finite-element model of the whole machine was solved. At 3000 r/min the no-load phase back-EMF has a fundamental of 288 V (rms). With the rated current in each phase, in phase with the back-EMF, the average torque is 34.7 N m. The slot fill factor is 0.42, and the rated current density is 6.2 A/mm2 (rms).

## 6. Conclusion

The machine meets its torque requirement at the rated current with a moderate current density. Its dimensions and materials are listed above.
