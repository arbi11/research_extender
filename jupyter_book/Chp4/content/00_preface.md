# Chapter 4: MDP Topology Optimization for Electrical Machines

This chapter develops SeqTO-v1, a sequential decision-making framework that reformulates topology optimization as a Markov Decision Process (MDP). The framework is demonstrated on a C-core electromagnetic actuator and evaluated against genetic algorithms.

## Chapter Overview

This chapter is organized as follows:

- **Section 1**: Introduction — topology optimization as sequential decision-making
- **Section 2**: Literature review of topology optimization methods and ML applications
- **Section 3**: MDP framework and Q-Learning
- **Section 4**: SeqTO-v1 — the C-core MDP environment
- **Section 5**: Genetic algorithms with SeqTO-v1
- **Section 6**: MDP implementation framework
- **Section 7**: Results, analysis, and future directions
