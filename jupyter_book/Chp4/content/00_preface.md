# Chapter 4: MDP Topology Optimization for Electrical Machines

This chapter explores the application of Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) and reinforcement learning techniques to the topology optimization of electrical machines, with a focus on Synchronous Reluctance Machines (SynRM).

## Chapter Overview

This chapter is organized as follows:

- **Section 1**: Introduction to topology optimization challenges in electrical machines
- **Section 2**: Comprehensive literature survey of existing approaches
- **Section 3**: Fundamentals of Markov Decision Processes
- **Section 4**: Q-Learning fundamentals and algorithms
- **Section 5**: SynRM environment modeling
- **Section 6**: Genetic algorithms as an alternative optimization approach
- **Section 7**: MDP implementation details
- **Section 8**: Comparison methodology between approaches
- **Section 9**: Results analysis and discussion
- **Section 10**: Advanced applications and future directions
